Thank you for considering a contribution to IVCT. The notes below
describe the process; please read them in full before opening a
pull request.

## Where to direct what

- **Bug reports and feature requests:** open an issue on GitHub.
- **Small fixes (typos, documentation, obvious bugs):** open a pull
  request directly.
- **Substantive changes (new features, refactoring):** please open
  an issue first to discuss the design before writing code, so we
  can confirm the change fits the framework's direction.
- **New plugins:** the framework deliberately keeps the catalogue
  of bundled plugins small. We are happy to link to community
  plugins from the README; please open an issue rather than a pull
  request.
- **Security issues:** please *do not* open a public issue. Email
  `support@cinteraction.com` so we can coordinate a fix and a
  disclosure timeline.

## Licensing of contributions

IVCT uses a two-tier licensing model (see `README.md` and `NOTICE`).
Contributions follow the license of the directory they touch:

- Contributions to the framework, services, and dashboards are
  accepted under **GPL-3.0-only**.
- Contributions to `plugins/face-count-service/` and `docs/plugin-author/`
  are accepted under **MIT**.

Every new source file should carry an `SPDX-License-Identifier`
header recording the applicable license.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

On your first contribution we will ask you to sign a Contributor
License Agreement. The CLA grants Cinteraction d.o.o. — as the
project's principal copyright holder — the right to relicense your
contribution under terms compatible with the project's two-tier
licensing model, including under an alternative commercial license
on terms equivalent to those offered for the framework as a whole.

The CLA does *not* assign copyright in your contribution to
Cinteraction. You retain copyright; you grant a broad licence. The
CLA exists for one reason: so that the project can continue to
offer the alternative commercial license described in `NOTICE`
without having to obtain individual consent from every contributor
whenever a commercial customer is signed on. Many open-source
projects in this space (Qt, MongoDB historically, MariaDB) operate
on the same model.

A copy of the CLA text is in `CLA.md`. The signing process is
handled through a bot on GitHub on your first pull request.

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, you may need to
have your employer sign a corporate variant of the CLA. We are
happy to discuss this; please email `[licensing@cinteraction.com]`.

## Coding standards

- Python: black, isort, ruff. CI will fail on formatting drift.
- JavaScript/TypeScript: prettier, eslint with the project config.
- SQL: lowercase keywords, two-space indent.
- Commits: imperative subject line, one logical change per commit.

## What we cannot accept

- Code under an incompatible license that we cannot reasonably
  relicense.
- Anonymous contributions, since the CLA requires an identifiable
  contributor.
- Code generated by AI assistants without a human reviewer who is
  prepared to vouch for it and sign the CLA on their own account.
  (We are not opposed to AI-assisted development; we just need a
  human author of record.)

## Recognition

All contributors are listed in `AUTHORS.md` after their first
merged pull request. If you would prefer to be listed under a
pseudonym, please indicate this in the pull request description.
